THE KERALAREADS IDEA
Our purpose

Make library management feel effortless.

Book details can easily get scattered across notebooks, spreadsheets and messages. KeralaReads brings the essentials together in a simple digital library where a collection can be browsed and its borrowing status can be tracked.

Built as a Progressive Web App, the project combines a familiar website experience with installable app features. Its layout is designed to stay clear and comfortable whether you open it on a laptop or a phone.

What guides the design

Simple by intention.

Useful features, a clean interface and a reading-first feel—without making the experience complicated.

🌿

Calm and clear

A warm library-inspired palette and uncluttered layouts keep the experience easy to navigate.

⚡

Practical tools

Search, filters and borrowing status help users find the right book and understand its availability.

📲

Ready to go

Installable PWA features help the site feel app-like on supported devices.

Behind the experience

Built with modern web foundations.

KeralaReads combines familiar front-end technologies with Firebase-backed library data.
